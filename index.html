    How to play

    1. Reach the burrow. Tap a tile with a ring to hop Pip one tile up, down, left or right. Gaps in the board can't be crossed by anyone.
    2. Read the clues. Each tile Pip lands on shows how many snares hide in the tiles around it, diagonals included. His ears twitch the same number of times.
    3. Avoid snares. Stepping on one costs a life. You have 3.
    4. Watch the rhythm. The Next hops strip shows which wolf moves after each of your hops. A faint orange paw marks where a wolf will step next.
    5. Set traps. Wolves can't see snares. Lead one across a hidden snare and it's stuck for 2 turns.
